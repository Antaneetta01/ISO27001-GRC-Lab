# Incident Response Plan

## Purpose

This incident response plan defines the process for identifying, managing, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents within the smart IoT infrastructure environment.

---

## Incident Response Objectives

The objectives of this plan are to:

* minimize organizational damage
* contain cybersecurity incidents
* restore normal operations
* improve incident handling procedures
* support governance and compliance activities

---

## Incident Categories

Examples of cybersecurity incidents include:

* malware infections
* unauthorized access
* phishing attacks
* denial of service attacks
* data breaches
* suspicious network activity

---

## Incident Response Phases

### 1. Identification

Security events are identified through:

* monitoring systems
* security logs
* user reports
* security alerts

---

### 2. Containment

Actions are taken to:

* isolate affected systems
* prevent further damage
* restrict unauthorized activity

---

### 3. Eradication

The organization removes:

* malicious files
* unauthorized access
* identified vulnerabilities

---

### 4. Recovery

Systems are:

* restored safely
* monitored for abnormal activity
* validated before returning to operations

---

### 5. Lessons Learned

After incidents:

* findings are documented
* response effectiveness is reviewed
* security improvements are identified

---

## Incident Reporting

All cybersecurity incidents should be:

* reported immediately
* documented appropriately
* escalated according to organizational procedures

---

## Governance Alignment

This incident response plan supports:

* ISO 27001 incident management concepts
* cybersecurity governance processes
* organizational resilience objectives
