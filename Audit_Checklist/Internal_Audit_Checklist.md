# Opportunities for Improvement

The following improvements are recommended to further enhance the project:

---

# Audit Conclusion

The internal audit confirms that the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project demonstrates a structured approach to Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) based on ISO 27001:2022 principles.

The project includes documented asset management, risk assessment, security policies, incident response planning, ISO 27001 control mapping, and supporting architecture documentation. The reviewed documentation provides evidence of a systematic approach to identifying, assessing, and managing cybersecurity risks within the simulated smart IoT environment.

The audit identified no major non-conformities within the documented scope. The recommended opportunities for improvement, including the development of a Statement of Applicability (SoA), a Business Continuity Plan, and periodic internal audit reviews, will further enhance the maturity of the project.

Overall, the project demonstrates a strong understanding of ISO 27001 governance concepts and serves as a practical portfolio for learning and applying Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) practices.
