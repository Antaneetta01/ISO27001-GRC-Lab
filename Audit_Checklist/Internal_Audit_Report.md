This Internal Audit Report summarizes the results of the simulated internal audit conducted for the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project. The report evaluates the implementation of governance, risk management, and information security controls against ISO 27001:2022 principles and identifies opportunities for continual improvement.

# Audit Objectives

The objectives of this audit were to:

- Review governance documentation.
- Verify implementation of risk management activities.
- Assess alignment with ISO 27001:2022 controls.
- Evaluate documentation supporting information security practices.
- Identify opportunities for continual improvement.

# Opportunities for Improvement

The following improvements are recommended to further enhance the maturity of the project:

1. Develop Third-Party Risk Assessment documentation for external service providers.

2. Create a Compliance Evidence Matrix to improve audit traceability and evidence management.

3. Implement a Vendor Security Assessment process to strengthen third-party risk management.

---

# Audit Conclusion

The simulated internal audit concludes that the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project demonstrates a structured approach to Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) aligned with ISO 27001:2022 principles.

The reviewed documentation provides evidence of asset management, risk assessment, policy development, incident response planning, business continuity planning, internal audit activities, ISO 27001:2022 control mapping, and the Statement of Applicability within the simulated smart IoT environment.

No major or minor non-conformities were identified within the scope of this simulated audit. The identified opportunities for improvement will further strengthen the project's governance, compliance, and third-party risk management capabilities.

Overall, the project demonstrates the practical application of ISO 27001:2022 governance concepts and serves as a structured learning portfolio for Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) within a simulated organizational environment.
