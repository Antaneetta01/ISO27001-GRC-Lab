# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the risk assessment methodology used within the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project for evaluating cybersecurity risks in a smart IoT infrastructure environment.

---

## Risk Assessment Objectives

The objectives of this methodology are to:

* identify organizational cybersecurity risks
* evaluate potential threats and vulnerabilities
* determine business impact
* prioritize risk treatment activities
* support governance and compliance initiatives

---

## Risk Identification

Risks are identified through:

* asset analysis
* threat modeling
* vulnerability assessment
* security incident analysis
* infrastructure review

---

## Risk Evaluation Criteria

Risks are evaluated using:

### Likelihood

The probability of a cybersecurity event occurring.

| Rating | Description    |
| ------ | -------------- |
| 1      | Rare           |
| 2      | Unlikely       |
| 3      | Possible       |
| 4      | Likely         |
| 5      | Almost Certain |

---

### Impact

The potential organizational damage caused by the risk.

| Rating | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| 1      | Negligible  |
| 2      | Minor       |
| 3      | Moderate    |
| 4      | Major       |
| 5      | Critical    |

---

## Risk Scoring

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

| Score Range | Risk Level |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| 1–5         | Low        |
| 6–10        | Medium     |
| 11–15       | High       |
| 16–25       | Critical   |

---

## Risk Treatment Options

Identified risks may be:

* mitigated
* transferred
* accepted
* avoided

---

## Governance Alignment

This methodology aligns with:

* ISO 27001 risk management principles
* NIST Cybersecurity Framework concepts
* organizational governance objectives

---

## Review Process

Risk assessments should be reviewed periodically to ensure continued effectiveness and alignment with organizational security requirements.
