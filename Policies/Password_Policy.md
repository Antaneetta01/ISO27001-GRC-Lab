# Password Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes password security requirements for protecting organizational systems, user accounts, and information assets within the smart IoT infrastructure environment.

---

## Password Requirements

Users shall create passwords that:

* contain at least 12 characters
* include uppercase and lowercase letters
* include numbers and special characters
* avoid easily guessable information
* are unique for each account

---

## Password Protection

Users shall:

* keep passwords confidential
* avoid password sharing
* avoid writing passwords in insecure locations
* use approved password management practices

---

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) should be enabled for:

* administrator accounts
* remote access systems
* critical organizational services

---

## Password Changes

Passwords should be changed:

* when compromise is suspected
* after security incidents
* according to organizational security requirements

---

## Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in restricted access to organizational systems and security review procedures.

---

## Governance Alignment

This policy supports:

* ISO 27001 access control principles
* organizational cybersecurity governance
* risk reduction initiatives
* secure authentication practices
