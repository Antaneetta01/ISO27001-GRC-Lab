# Business Continuity Plan

# Purpose

The purpose of this Business Continuity Plan (BCP) is to establish a structured approach for maintaining and restoring critical business operations during and after disruptive events affecting the simulated smart IoT infrastructure environment used within the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project.

This plan supports organizational resilience and aligns with ISO 27001:2022 business continuity principles.

---

# Scope

This Business Continuity Plan applies to the following critical assets and services:

* IoT Gateway
* Smart Sensors
* Security Log Server
* Building Management Dashboard
* Administrator Accounts
* Network Infrastructure
* Cloud Services

---

# Business Continuity Objectives

The objectives of this plan are to:

* Maintain availability of critical business services.
* Minimize operational disruption.
* Protect organizational information assets.
* Restore critical services within acceptable timeframes.
* Support incident recovery activities.
* Improve organizational resilience.

# Potential Business Disruptions

Examples of disruptive events include:

* Malware infection
* Denial of Service (DoS) attack
* Unauthorized access
* Network outage
* Cloud service disruption
* Hardware failure
* Power outage

---

# Business Continuity Strategy

To maintain business operations, the organization should:

* Maintain regular system backups.
* Implement redundancy for critical services where appropriate.
* Monitor critical systems continuously.
* Follow documented incident response procedures.
* Restore services according to business priorities.
* Verify system integrity before returning to normal operations.

---

# Backup Strategy

The organization should:

* Perform regular backups of critical data.
* Protect backup copies from unauthorized access.
* Periodically verify backup integrity.
* Maintain secure backup storage.

# Communication During Disruptions

During a disruption, the organization should:

* Notify relevant stakeholders.
* Communicate recovery status.
* Escalate critical incidents when required.
* Document recovery activities.

* Reviewed annually.
* Updated after significant organizational changes.
* Reviewed following major cybersecurity incidents.
* Improved through periodic testing and lessons learned.
