# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish information security governance principles for protecting organizational information assets within a smart IoT infrastructure environment.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

* organizational systems
* IoT devices
* network infrastructure
* users and administrators
* information assets

---

## Security Objectives

The organization aims to:

* protect confidentiality of information
* maintain integrity of systems and data
* ensure availability of critical services
* reduce cybersecurity risk exposure
* support regulatory and governance compliance

---

## Access Control

Access to organizational systems shall be:

* role-based
* authorized
* monitored
* periodically reviewed

---

## Password Requirements

Users shall:

* use strong passwords
* avoid password sharing
* enable multi-factor authentication where applicable

---

## Incident Reporting

Security incidents shall be:

* reported immediately
* documented appropriately
* investigated according to incident response procedures

---

## Compliance

Security controls shall align with:

* ISO 27001
* NIST Cybersecurity Framework

---

## Review

This policy shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued effectiveness and governance alignment.
