# Statement of Applicability (SoA)

## Purpose

The Statement of Applicability (SoA) identifies the ISO 27001:2022 controls applicable to the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project. It documents the applicability of selected controls, the justification for their implementation, their implementation status, and the supporting project documentation.

The SoA demonstrates how the simulated smart IoT environment aligns with ISO 27001:2022 information security requirements and supports Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) activities.

This Statement of Applicability demonstrates how the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project aligns with selected ISO 27001:2022 controls within the simulated smart IoT environment.

Together, these documents demonstrate a structured approach to Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC).

The Statement of Applicability supports audit readiness, improves traceability between ISO 27001 controls and project documentation, and provides evidence of the project's security governance implementation.
