## Purpose

This document maps governance and cybersecurity documentation within the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project to relevant ISO 27001:2022 controls.

The mapping demonstrates how governance and security documentation support:

* cybersecurity governance
* risk management
* security control implementation
* compliance readiness
* organizational security improvement

## Governance Alignment

This project aligns with key ISO 27001 governance concepts including:

* asset management
* risk assessment and treatment
* access control
* authentication security
* vulnerability management
* logging and monitoring
* incident management
* business continuity support
* policy governance
* compliance management

## Benefits of Control Mapping

Control mapping helps organizations:

* understand how security controls support business objectives
* identify gaps in governance and compliance processes
* demonstrate alignment with ISO 27001 requirements
* support audit and compliance activities
* improve overall cybersecurity maturity
