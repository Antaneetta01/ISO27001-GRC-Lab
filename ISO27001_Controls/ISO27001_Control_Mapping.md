## Purpose

This document maps governance, risk management, and cybersecurity documentation within the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project to relevant ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls.

The mapping demonstrates how documented policies, procedures, risk management activities, incident response processes, business continuity planning, and security governance support the simulated Information Security Management System (ISMS).

---

## Control Mapping Approach

The control mapping was developed based on the scope and security requirements of the simulated smart IoT infrastructure environment.

Each selected control is linked to one or more project documents that provide supporting evidence of the corresponding governance or security activity.

The mapping helps establish traceability between:

**ISO 27001 Control → Security Requirement → Project Documentation → Supporting Evidence**

## Key Control Areas

### Access Management

Access management controls are supported through the Information Security Policy and Privileged Access Management Procedure.

The project addresses:

- Least privilege
- Need-to-know access
- Access approval
- Privileged account management
- Periodic access reviews
- Access modification
- Access revocation
- Separation of duties
- Multi-factor authentication where supported

### Privileged Access Management

The Privileged Access Management Procedure establishes documented requirements for managing administrator and other privileged accounts.

The procedure addresses:

- Privileged account provisioning
- Administrator account requirements
- Least privilege
- Privileged access approval
- Periodic privileged access review
- Privileged activity logging
- Monitoring
- Emergency privileged access
- Access revocation

### Incident Management

The Incident Response Plan and Incident Response Workflow support the management of information security incidents through:

- Identification
- Assessment
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery
- Lessons learned

### Business Continuity

The Business Continuity Plan supports continuity and recovery of critical services within the simulated environment.

It addresses:

- Critical business services
- Recovery priorities
- Backup strategy
- Recovery activities
- Roles and responsibilities
- Communication during disruptions
- Plan maintenance

### Vulnerability Management

The Risk Register supports the identification, assessment, prioritization, and treatment of cybersecurity risks associated with technical vulnerabilities.

## Scope and Limitation

This control mapping represents selected ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls relevant to the simulated smart IoT environment used in the ISO27001-GRC-Lab project.

It is a portfolio and learning artifact and does not represent a complete organizational ISO 27001 control assessment or certification.

---

## Conclusion

The ISO 27001:2022 Control Mapping provides traceability between selected Annex A controls and the governance and cybersecurity documentation developed for the simulated smart IoT environment.

The mapping demonstrates how access management, privileged access, authentication, vulnerability management, incident response, logging, monitoring, business continuity, and compliance activities can be documented within a simulated ISMS.

The control mapping supports audit readiness, evidence organization, and continual improvement of the project's Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) processes.
